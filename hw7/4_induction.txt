
Use mathematical induction to prove that the sum of the first n even natural numbers equals n(n+1).
That is, that:

    2 + 4 + ... + 2n = n(n+1)

(Note that we are considering "2" to be the first even natural number.)

claim: for every natural number n >= 1

2 + 4 + ... + 2n = n(n+1)

base case (n = 1)
the left side is just 2 the right side is 1(1+1) = 2
claim therefore holds for n = 1

Inductive Hypothesis
assume the claim holds
2 + 4 + ... + 2k=k(k+1)

Inductive step
2 + 4 + ... + 2k=k(k+1) = (k+1)(k+2)
                = k(k+1)+2(k+1)
                =(k+1)(k+2)

claim is true for n = 1 and whenever it is true for k, it is true for k + 1
