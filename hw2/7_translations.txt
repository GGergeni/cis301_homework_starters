Suppose you have the following propositional atoms:

p: It is sunny tomorrow
q: I will go hiking
r: I carry an umbrella
s: I am tired
t: I eat an apple


Express each of these propositional logic statements in English as
succinctly as you can.


Statement 1: p → ¬r ∧ q

if it is sunny tommorow, I will not carry an umbrella and I will go hicking.


Statement 2: ¬(r ∧ t) → ¬q

If do not carry an umbrella and eat an apple, then I wont go hicking  


Statement 3: s → (¬p → ¬q)
if I am tired, and its not sunny then I wont go hicking.
