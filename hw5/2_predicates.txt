We have the following set of students:

--------------------------------------------------------|
Name    | Major     | KS resident   | Lives on-campus   |
--------------------------------------------------------|
Bob     | PHYS      | True          | False             |
--------------------------------------------------------|
Ava     | ECE       | True          | True              |
--------------------------------------------------------|
Jane    | CS        | False         | False             |
--------------------------------------------------------|
Steve   | MATH      | True          | True              |
--------------------------------------------------------|
Marie   | CS        | False         | True              |
--------------------------------------------------------|
Jack    | ECE       | True          | False             |
--------------------------------------------------------|

And the following predicates over our set of students:

IsCS(x) : whether student x is a CS major

InKS(x): whether student x is a Kansas resident

Campus(x): whether student x lives on campus



Consider the following predicate logic statements:

1) ∀ x (¬InCS(x))

    a) Express the statement in words
    Every student is not a CS major




    b) Is the statement true or false in our set of students? Explain.
    False Jane is a cs major 



///

2) ∃ x (¬Campus(x) ⋀ InKS(x))

    a) Express the statement in words
    there is at least on student who does not live on campus and is a kansas resident




    b) Is the statement true or false in our set of students? Explain.

    true: bob is a kansas resident who lives off campus


///

3) ∀ x (¬Campus(x) → InCS(x))

    a) Express the statement in words
    every off campus student is a Cs Major




    b) Is the statement true or false in our set of students? Explain.
    False Bob lives off campus



///

4) ¬(∃ x (Campus(x) ⋀ InCS(x)))

    a) Express the statement in words
    there is no student who both lives on campus and is a CS major



    b) Is the statement true or false in our set of students? Explain.
    False Marie is a CS major and lives on campus



///

5) ¬(∀ x (InCS(x) → Campus(x)))

   a) Express the statement in words
    not every CS major lives on campus 



   b) Is the statement true or false in our set of students? Explain.
    True Jane is a CS major who lives on campus
